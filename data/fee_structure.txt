# DHARMSINH DESAI UNIVERSITY (DDU) - ANNUAL FEE STRUCTURE & PAYMENT RULES

## 1. Regulatory Body (Fee Regulatory Committee - FRC)
Tuition fees for all professional self-financed programs at DDU are determined and approved periodically by the **Fee Regulatory Committee (Technical / Medical), Government of Gujarat (FRC Gujarat)**.

## 2. Undergraduate Programs Fee Breakdown (Indicative Annual Rates)
- **B.Tech (Grant-in-Aid Seats - CE, CH, IC, CL, MH)**:
  - Approx. **₹1,500 to ₹3,500 per year** (Subsidized by the Government of Gujarat for GIA quota).
- **B.Tech (Self-Financed Seats - IT, CE-SFI, EC-SFI)**:
  - Approx. **₹1,52,000 to ₹1,68,000 per year** (payable in two equal semester installments of ₹76,000 to ₹84,000 per semester).
- **B.Pharm (Bachelor of Pharmacy)**:
  - Approx. **₹1,40,000 to ₹1,55,000 per year**.
- **BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)**:
  - Approx. **₹45,000 to ₹60,000 per year**.
- **BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)**:
  - Approx. **₹45,000 to ₹60,000 per year**.
- **BDS (Bachelor of Dental Surgery - Dental College)**:
  - Approx. **₹3,20,000 to ₹3,80,000 per year** (Govt/Management quota as determined by FRC Medical).

## 3. Postgraduate Programs Fee Breakdown
- **MCA (Master of Computer Applications - 2 Years)**:
  - Approx. **₹95,000 to ₹1,10,000 per year** (payable semester-wise).
- **MBA (Master of Business Administration - 2 Years)**:
  - Approx. **₹1,10,000 to ₹1,25,000 per year**.
- **M.Tech Programs**:
  - Approx. **₹1,20,000 to ₹1,40,000 per year** (AICTE stipend of ₹12,400/month available for GATE-qualified candidates).
- **M.Pharm / MDS**:
  - As per FRC guidelines (M.Pharm approx. ₹1.6L - ₹1.8L/yr; MDS as per specialty clinical matrix).

## 4. Ancillary & One-Time Fees
- **Refundable Caution Money Deposit**: ₹5,000 (one-time upon initial admission, refunded at program completion).
- **Library & University Enrolment Fee**: ₹2,000 - ₹3,000.
- **Examination Fee**: ₹1,500 - ₹2,500 per regular semester.

## 5. Mode of Payment & Late Fee Penalty
- Tuition and semester fees must be paid online via the DDU NetBanking / UPI / Payment Gateway on the student portal or by Demand Draft in favor of "Dharmsinh Desai University" payable at Nadiad.
- Fees must be remitted within **15 days** of semester commencement. A late penalty fee of ₹50 to ₹100 per day is applicable after the due date.
