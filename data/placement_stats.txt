# DHARMSINH DESAI UNIVERSITY (DDU) - TRAINING & PLACEMENT (T&P) CELL & CAREER STATISTICS

## 1. Overview of DDU Training & Placement Cell
DDU Nadiad has an exceptional, longstanding placement legacy across Engineering, Computer Applications, Pharmacy, and Management. The University maintains strong industrial partnerships with multinational tech giants, core chemical conglomerates, fintech firms, and premier consulting companies.

## 2. Key Placement Statistics & Packages
- **Highest International / Domestic CTC Package**: ₹35.00 LPA to ₹44.00 LPA (Amazon, Atlassian, Microsoft, Google off-campus / national drives).
- **Average Package (IT / Computer Engineering / MCA)**: ₹7.50 LPA to ₹9.20 LPA.
- **Average Package (Chemical / EC / IC / Mechanical)**: ₹5.50 LPA to ₹6.80 LPA.
- **Overall Median CTC**: ₹6.50 LPA.
- **Placement Percentage**: 88% - 95% of eligible registered students placed annually in IT, CE, and Chemical branches.

## 4. Placement Eligibility Rules & Guidelines
1. **Academic Cutoff**: Most Tier-1 software and core companies specify a minimum of **6.5 to 7.0 CPI** with no active backlogs at the time of the drive.
2. **Backlog Policy**:
   - Students with **more than one active backlog (ATKT)** are strictly ineligible to register for on-campus placement drives until the backlogs are cleared.
   - For dream/super-dream companies (> ₹10 LPA CTC), companies generally enforce a strict **0 active backlog & 0 historical backlog** requirement.
3. **One Student One Job (Dream Offer) Policy**:
   - Once a student receives an initial job offer up to ₹5.5 LPA, they are considered placed.
   - A placed student is permitted to appear for a "Dream Company" drive if the offering CTC is at least 1.75x to 2x higher than their existing offer. Once a dream offer is accepted, the candidate exits subsequent drives.
4. **Discipline & Dress Code**:
   - Formal university attire, valid student ID card, and updated resume are mandatory for all interview rounds. Unethical conduct, proxy interviews, or unannounced absence after shortlisting leads to permanent blacklisting from T&P assistance.

## 5. 8th Semester Industry Internship Policy
- B.Tech final year (8th Semester) students are encouraged and permitted to undertake a full-semester (6 months) industrial internship in reputed IT companies or core industries.
- The university grants credits for the 6-month industry project subject to periodic evaluations, mentor reviews, and end-semester project viva voce examinations.
