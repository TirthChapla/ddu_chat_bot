# DHARMSINH DESAI UNIVERSITY (DDU) - ACADEMIC ATTENDANCE REGULATIONS & POLICY

## 1. Minimum Mandatory Attendance Requirement
According to the official Academic Regulations of Dharmsinh Desai University (DDU), Nadiad:
- Every regular student enrolled in undergraduate (B.Tech, B.Pharm, BDS), postgraduate (M.Tech, MCA, MBA, M.Pharm, MDS), and diploma programs must maintain a **minimum of 75% attendance** in each registered theory course (lectures) and each practical / laboratory session during the semester.
- Attendance is recorded on a per-period basis by course instructors and uploaded into the university student management portal weekly.

## 2. Attendance Shortage & Debarment Policy
- Any student failing to secure a minimum of 75% attendance in a specific course (theory or practical) will be declared **DEBARRED (Detained)** from appearing in the Semester End University Examination (external exam) for that particular course.
- Debarred students will receive a grade of 'X' or 'NA' (Not Admitted to exam) for that course and must re-register and attend the course whenever it is offered in subsequent semesters to complete their degree requirements.

## 3. Condonation of Attendance Shortage on Medical / Exceptional Grounds
- Under exceptional circumstances, such as genuine medical illness requiring hospitalization or representation of the university in recognized national/state-level sports/cultural/technical events:
  - The Dean / Head of Department (HOD) upon the recommendation of the Faculty Advisor may condone attendance shortage up to a **maximum of 10%** (i.e. bringing the required threshold down from 75% to 65%).
  - **No condonation is permitted below 65% attendance under any circumstances**.
- **Procedure to apply for Medical Condonation**:
  1. The student or their parent/guardian must submit a formal application accompanied by a valid medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner / hospital within **3 working days** of returning to campus.
  2. The university medical officer / designated health committee reviews and validates the authenticity of medical records.
  3. Final approval is granted by the Faculty Dean prior to the issuance of examination hall tickets.

## 4. Monitoring & Notification to Parents
- Monthly attendance lists are published on the departmental notice boards and sent via SMS/portal alerts to parents/guardians.
- Warning letters are issued at mid-semester (after the 1st internal sessional exam) to students having attendance below 70%.

## 5. Summary Key Points
- Minimum Required: **75%** (Theory & Practicals separately).
- Medical Condonation Limit: Up to **10% relaxation** (Minimum **65%** required with valid proof).
- Less than 65%: Strictly Debarred (No exam hall ticket for the subject).
- Source Reference: *DDU Academic Regulations & Student Handbook, Clause 4.1-4.8*.
