# DHARMSINH DESAI UNIVERSITY (DDU) - SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

## 1. MYSY (Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana - Gujarat Government)
- **Eligibility**:
  - Gujarat domicile student who secured **80 percentile or above** in 10th (for Diploma to Degree) or 12th Board Examination (HSC Science).
  - Annual family income must not exceed **₹6,00,000 (6 Lakhs) per annum**.
- **Scholarship Benefits for DDU Students**:
  - **Tuition Fee Assistance**: 50% of the tuition fee or a maximum of **₹50,000 per year** (whichever is less) for Degree Engineering (B.Tech), Pharmacy, Dental, etc.
  - **Hostel & Food Assistance**: ₹1,200 per month (up to ₹12,000 per year for 10 months) for students residing in hostels outside their native taluka.
  - **Book & Equipment Allowance**: One-time grant of ₹10,000 for degree engineering / medical / dental courses.

## 2. Freeship Card Scheme & Digital Gujarat Scholarships for SC / ST / SEBC
- **Freeship Card (SC / ST Category)**:
  - Eligible SC / ST students possessing a valid Freeship Card issued by the Social Justice & Empowerment Department, Government of Gujarat, are exempted from upfront tuition fee payment. The government reimburses 100% of approved tuition fees directly.
  - Family income limit: Up to ₹2,50,000 per annum for SC / ST Post-Matric schemes.
- **Digital Gujarat Post-Matric Scholarship (SEBC / OBC / NT-DNT / EWS)**:
  - Online application via the `digitalgujarat.gov.in` portal.
  - Provides tuition fee reimbursement and maintenance allowances based on annual family income ceilings.

## 3. Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS - ACPC)
- 5% supernumerary seats in each branch at DDU are earmarked under the **AICTE Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS)**.
- Awarded strictly on ACPC merit rank to students with family income less than ₹8,00,000 per annum.
- 100% tuition fee is waived for all 4 years of the B.Tech program.

## 4. DDU Institutional Merit Scholarships & Alumnus Funds
- Dharmsinh Desai University awards annual merit certificates and cash prizes to branch toppers and semester gold medalists.
- Endowed trust funds and DDU Alumni Association (DDUAA) grant need-cum-merit financial aid to deserving students facing unforeseen economic hardships.

## 5. Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship & AICTE Pragati / Saksham
- National Scholarship Portal (NSP) schemes for higher education.
- AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girl Students admitted to AICTE-approved technical degree programs (₹50,000 per year assistance).
- AICTE Saksham Scholarship for differently-abled students.
- AICTE PG GATE Stipend: ₹12,400 per month for GATE-qualified M.Tech candidates.
