# DHARMSINH DESAI UNIVERSITY (DDU) - HOSTELS & CAMPUS FACILITIES

## 1. On-Campus Student Accommodation
DDU provides well-furnished and secure residential facilities for outstation students:
- **Boys' Hostels**: Multi-storey hostel blocks with single, double, and triple-sharing occupancy rooms.
- **Girls' Hostels**: Dedicated high-security girls' hostel complex with biometric access, CCTV monitoring, and residential lady wardens.

## 2. Hostel Fees & Inclusions (Indicative Annual Rates)
- **Hostel Rent / Room Fee**: Approx. **₹24,000 to ₹36,000 per academic year** (varies by room occupancy and air-cooled/regular options).
- **Refundable Hostel Caution Deposit**: ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 (one-time).
- **Mess & Dining Charges**: Approx. **₹3,000 to ₹4,000 per month** (operated on cooperative/contract basis providing clean, hygienic vegetarian breakfast, lunch, and dinner).

## 3. Hostel Amenities & Infrastructure
- High-speed campus Wi-Fi network and 24x7 electricity backup.
- RO purified drinking water stations and solar water heating systems.
- Common recreation rooms equipped with television, table tennis, carrom, and daily newspapers.
- On-campus gymnasium and sports grounds for cricket, volleyball, badminton, and basketball.
- 24/7 round-the-clock security personnel and emergency medical assistance connected to the university health clinic.

## 4. Hostel Rules, Discipline & Curfew Timings
- **Curfew / In-Time**:
  - Girls' Hostel: Gates close at **8:00 PM** in evening. Prior written permission from the Warden is required for late reporting or outstation visits.
  - Boys' Hostel: Gates close at **10:00 PM**.
- **Night Outs / Leave Application**:
  - Students must submit online leave requests or physical leave slips counter-signed by parents/guardians before leaving campus for weekends/vacations.
- **Strict Prohibitions**:
  - Ragging is strictly banned with zero-tolerance (punishable under UGC anti-ragging act with expulsion and FIR).
  - Smoking, alcohol consumption, unauthorized electrical heating appliances, and possession of motorized vehicles without parking permits are strictly prohibited.

## 5. Library & Computing Facilities
- **Central Library**: Multi-storey library holding over 1,00,000 books, subscriptions to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, Springer, ACM Digital Library, and air-conditioned reading halls open till late evening.
- **Central Computing Center (CCC)**: High-performance workstations with gigabit fiber backbone connectivity.
