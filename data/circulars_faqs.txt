# DHARMSINH DESAI UNIVERSITY (DDU) - CIRCULARS, NOTICES & STUDENT FAQS

## 1. Academic Calendar & Term Schedules (General Cycle)
- **Odd Semester (Sem 1, 3, 5, 7)**:
  - Commencement of Teaching: Mid-July to early August.
  - 1st Sessional Examination: September (after 6-7 weeks of teaching).
  - 2nd Sessional Examination: October / November.
  - Term End & Practical Exams: Late November.
  - University Semester End Theory Examinations: December.
  - Winter Vacation: Late December (approx. 2-3 weeks).
- **Even Semester (Sem 2, 4, 6, 8)**:
  - Commencement of Teaching: January.
  - Sessional Exams: February & April.
  - Semester End Examinations: May / June.
  - Summer Vacation / Industry Internship: June to mid-July.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
**Q: How do I apply for an official Bonafide Certificate or Transcript?**
A: Students must fill out the Bonafide / Transcript Application Form available at the Student Section (Admin Block) or online student portal, pay the nominal processing fee (₹100 for Bonafide, ₹500 per set of Transcripts), and collect the sealed document within 3-5 working days.

**Q: What is the procedure for obtaining a Duplicate Marksheet or Degree Certificate?**
A: Submit an application with a copy of the police FIR (for lost certificate), a notarized indemnity affidavit, photo ID proof, and fee receipt at the Examination Controller's office.

**Q: Is bus transportation available for day scholars commuting from Ahmedabad, Vadodara, or Anand?**
A: Yes, private contract bus operators and GSRTC student concession passes provide convenient daily commuting routes connecting Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Anand, and nearby towns directly to the DDU Nadiad campus.

**Q: Can students participate in hackathons, robotics competitions, and sports fests?**
A: Yes! DDU actively supports student participation in national hackathons (Smart India Hackathon - SIH), IEEE conferences, Felicity / TechFest, and AIU inter-university sports events. OD (On-Duty) attendance is granted for authorized student teams.
